About the shop

A trusted chair in the middle of Katy

Professional Hair Salon - Katy has become a regular stop for men and families across Katy, TX who want a haircut done properly — not rushed. Every visit starts with a real conversation about what you want, and ends with a cut that actually holds its shape past the parking lot.

Our barbers and stylists bring years of hands-on experience to every fade, trim and shampoo, and we keep the shop clean, comfortable and unhurried, so the twenty minutes in the chair feel like they're worth it.

  • Experienced barbers & stylists
  • Detail-focused finishing work
  • Clean, welcoming shop
  • Consistent results, visit after visit

What we do

Services

A working list of what's in regular rotation in the chair — ask your barber if you don't see exactly what you're after.

  • Classic Haircut A precise, made-to-face cut with clippers and shears, finished with a clean neckline.
  • Skin Fade A seamless blend down to the skin, tapered and detailed at the hairline.
  • Beard Trim Shaped, lined and softened, with a hot towel finish on request.
  • Hair Styling Blow-dry and finish work for a polished look that lasts, on any hair type.
  • Shampoo & Cut A relaxing wash to start, followed by your cut and a light style.
  • Kids Haircut Patient, friendly cuts for younger clients, ages 10 and under.
  • Senior Haircut An unhurried, comfortable cut with a little extra time built in.
  • Hair & Beard Package The full service — haircut, beard shape-up and finishing touches together.
